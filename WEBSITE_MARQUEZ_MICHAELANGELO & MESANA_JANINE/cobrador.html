COBRADOR ISLAND

Cobrador Island, locally known as Naguso Island, is the northernmost and largest of the three islets off the coast of Romblon. This tranquil, 300-hectare island is inhabited by a small fishing and farming community and is celebrated for its untouched charm, featuring powdery white sand beaches made of crushed coral, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and thriving marine sanctuaries. It serves as a premier destination for island hopping, snorkeling, and cliff jumping, offering a peaceful escape far from heavy commercialization.
