CRESTA DE GALLO

Cresta de Gallo is a secluded, kidney-shaped islet located off the coast of San Fernando on Sibuyan Island, Romblon. Often described as a "hidden paradise," it is famous for its pristine white sandbars and vibrant cerulean waters that remain largely untouched by commercial tourism. The island's name, Spanish for "rooster's crest," refers to its unique silhouette, which features jagged rock formations and a small hill that resemble the comb of a rooster when viewed from a distance.
