TINAGONG DAGAT

Tinagong Dagat (meaning "Hidden Sea") specifically refers to a unique saltwater lake located at Kabibihan Point in the municipality of Calatrava on the northern tip of Tablas Island. It is composed of a main semi-circular lake and a smaller second lake, both of which are naturally carved into limestone rock formations and concealed by tall, jagged cliffs. Because the entrance is hidden from the open sea, the area remained a secret hideout for rebels during World War II before becoming a scenic tourist destination.

The most distinctive feature of this site is its geological connection to the ocean through underground channels, which creates a paradoxical tidal phenomenon: when the tide is high in the surrounding sea, the water level in the lake is low, and vice versa. While primarily visited for its breathtaking limestone scenery and tranquil atmosphere, it is also a popular spot for adventure seekers interested in cliff jumping and island-hopping tours starting from the port of Calatrava.
