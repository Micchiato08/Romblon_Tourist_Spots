MOUNT GUITING-GUITING

Mount Guiting-Guiting, located on Sibuyan Island in the province of Romblon, Philippines, is one of the most formidable and biologically diverse mountains in Southeast Asia. Standing at 2,058 meters above sea level, its name translates to "jagged" in the local dialect, a direct reference to the serrated, saw-tooth profile of its rocky ridges. Often referred to as the "Galapagos of Asia," the mountain is the centerpiece of a protected natural park that hosts a high concentration of endemic flora and fauna, many of which are found nowhere else on Earth due to the island's long-term isolation from the mainland.

For the mountaineering community, "G2" is legendary for its extreme technical difficulty and unpredictable weather. The trail is famous for landmarks like the "Knife Edge" ridge—a narrow, rocky spine with steep drops on both sides—and "Kiss the Wall," where climbers must navigate a vertical rock face with minimal footings. Despite the grueling three-day trek and the physical toll of its nearly vertical ascents, the mountain remains a "bucket list" destination for seasoned hikers drawn to its raw, prehistoric beauty and the unparalleled views of the Sibuyan Sea from its jagged summit.
